WORK IN PROGRESS!!!

Takes specifically-named bones and applies some extra functionality to them such as "look at" constraints.

Examples:
	Snudio.LookAt.Bottom.Trapezius_L
	Snudio.LookAt.Top.Trapezius_L

These two bones will be pointed at each other.

Must be used *after* PoseIK, or the rotations will not be accurate.
